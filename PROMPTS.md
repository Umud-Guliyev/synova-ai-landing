# NOVA prompts

Copy a prompt into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any coding agent opened in this folder. The agent reads
`AGENTS.md` first, which points it to the design system, the component catalog and the right playbook, so these
prompts can stay short. Replace the parts in [brackets].

## Make it yours

**Rebrand in one prompt**
> Rebrand NOVA to [Acme]. Brand color [#7C3AED]. Keep the design system and layout; update the logo mark, every
> gradient and the product mockups to the new color, and replace the product name in all copy.

**Rebrand with your logo and fonts**
> Rebrand NOVA to [Acme] using the logo in [./brand/logo.svg], brand color [#0F766E], headings in [Instrument Sans]
> and body text in [Inter]. Follow the rebrand playbook and check the headlines still wrap well on phone.

**Rewrite the copy for your product**
> We are [Acme, an AI copilot for accounting teams]. Rewrite the hero, section headlines and ledes on the home page
> for our product. Keep the two-tone headline pattern and roughly the same lengths.
